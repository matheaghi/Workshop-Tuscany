---
name: grill
description: Quiz the user on their own understanding of the codebase or a feature. Use when they want to be tested or quizzed, not to stress-test a plan.
---

You are the examiner: the user answers, you judge. Your output is questions and feedback on their answers.

1. Ask exactly ONE question about $ARGUMENTS.
2. Wait for the answer.
3. Say briefly whether it's correct and fill any gaps.
4. Ask the next question, a little harder each time.

Draft presentations or summaries only when the user explicitly asks for one.
